# Facilitator guide — PMO First Principles

**Audience:** People asked to lead cross-functional work without a formal project-management role.

**Format:** Ten independent 20–30-minute sessions or five paired 60-minute workshops. This is original, informal educational material, not a university course or professional credential.

## Each session
1. 3 minutes: describe the business problem and learning outcome.
2. 7 minutes: explain the core principle using a fictional scenario.
3. 10 minutes: complete a worksheet in pairs.
4. 5 minutes: discuss assumptions and decision points.
5. 3 minutes: knowledge check and takeaway.

## Facilitator prompts
- What decision is missing?
- What evidence would prove the outcome?
- Who will own the result after launch?
- Which assumption creates the greatest risk?
- What would you stop doing to free the capacity?

## Capstone: fictional service onboarding improvement
Learners use one scenario across the whole course. A fictional support team receives inconsistent new-hire equipment requests, causing rework and long lead times. The sponsor wants a measurable improvement without deploying an entirely new platform.

Deliver: one-page charter; stakeholder plan; milestone and dependency plan; RAID log; weekly status report; change decision; acceptance/handoff plan. See [assessment rubric](capstone-rubric.md).

## Evidence-based completion
Knowledge checks test recognition, not practical competence. Use the capstone rubric and an observed conversation before saying a learner can reliably apply the practices.

## Safe publication
Never use real employee, student, customer, patient, or employer-specific project data in public exercises. Avoid uploading institutional templates without appropriate rights.

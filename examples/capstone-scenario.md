# Fictional capstone: Reliable new-hire equipment onboarding

The Service Enablement Team supports approximately 100 new hires each month. A fictional baseline shows 18% of equipment requests require rework, and the median time from approved request to ready-to-use equipment is 9 working days.

A sponsor has asked for a 90-day improvement pilot targeting fewer than 10% reworked requests and a median lead time of 6 working days. Budget for new systems is limited; the team must use its existing request and inventory systems.

## Constraints
- Identity, purchasing, and endpoint teams each have competing workloads
- The support team needs a documented ownership handoff
- A cybersecurity review is required before any workflow change is released
- A proposed automated intake form would add three weeks to the pilot

## Learner tasks
1. Draft the charter and identify the accountable sponsor.
2. Describe three deliverables and two exclusions.
3. Sequence milestones and record two dependencies.
4. Identify one risk, existing issue, assumption and decision.
5. Document a change request for the automated form.
6. Draft a status update showing evidence and decision needed.
7. Define operating ownership and a 30/90-day benefits review.

All people, figures, targets, and systems in this exercise are invented.
